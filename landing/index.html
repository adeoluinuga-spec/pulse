Trust

Designed for the most sensitive information you hold.

Performance ratings, candid feedback and salaries. Pulse treats each of them that way.

Your organisation, sealed off

Every record belongs to one organisation, and every request is checked against it on the server — not only in the database.

Feedback stays anonymous

Rater groups too small to protect identity are hidden. Comments are pseudonymised, and exports never link a comment to a name.

Salaries stay private

Pay is invisible to colleagues at the database level. Each person sees only their own payslips.

Two people for every payment

Anyone who prepared or adjusted a payroll run cannot approve it. Approved runs, and the bank details they pay to, are frozen.

A record of every decision

Releases, approvals, report access and exports are logged, so you can always answer "who did this, and when?"

People decide, not AI

AI drafts summaries and suggestions. It never sets a score, releases a result or approves pay.

Questions

Before you ask.

Do we have to use every part of Pulse?

No. Many organisations start with a 360 assessment or an appraisal cycle and add planning, payroll or the team workspace later. Everything you add connects to what is already there.

Do people giving 360 feedback need a Pulse account?

No. Each rater gets a private, single-use link by email. It works on a phone, saves as they go, and lets them pick up where they left off.

Can a manager see who said what in a 360?

No. Anonymous groups — colleagues, direct reports and customers — are only shown when there are enough responses to protect identity, and written comments are never attached to a name.

Does Pulse pay salaries or file our taxes?

No. Pulse calculates pay, takes it through independent approval, and produces the bank and remittance schedules your finance team uploads. Money never moves through Pulse, and returns are not filed from it.

Our structure is unusual. Will Pulse fit?

Pulse reads your own org chart and lets you plan in as many levels as you use, with the names you use. Appraisal weights and 360 frameworks are yours to set. If something does not fit, tell us when you request access.

What does the AI actually do?

It summarises and suggests: coaching based on your own goals and reports, themes from 360 comments written to protect anonymity, and team summaries. It never sets scores, releases results or approves anything.

Can staff use Pulse on their phones?

Yes. Pulse works in the browser on phones, tablets and computers, and 360 review links are designed for phones first.
