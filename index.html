✦ The problem

Performance management is broken. Everyone knows it.

Annual appraisals feel arbitrary. Employees don't know where they stand. Managers are too busy to give real feedback. HR is stuck chasing paperwork. Pulse fixes all of it.

😤
Employees are in the dark
Most employees only find out how they're performing once a year — at their appraisal. By then it's too late to change anything. Pulse gives every employee a live score, every day.
📋
Qualitative reports go nowhere
Employees spend time writing weekly reports that managers don't have time to read properly. Critical information — blockers, frustrations, wins — gets lost. Pulse's AI reads every word and surfaces what matters.
⚖️
Appraisals feel unfair
When scoring is opaque, employees feel judged on who they are, not what they've done. Pulse makes every component of every score visible and explainable — to both the employee and their manager.
🔇
Struggling employees go unnoticed
High performers get attention. Struggling employees get overlooked until it's too late. Pulse flags risk early and privately recommends collaboration — before performance becomes a crisis.
🗂️
HR is buried in admin
HR teams spend months coordinating appraisals, chasing submissions, and compiling data manually. Pulse automates the process end-to-end and gives HR a live view of every employee, every cycle.
💸
Performance isn't linked to pay
Most employees don't know what their performance score means for their bonus — until they receive it. Pulse shows every employee exactly what their score is worth, in real numbers, in real time.

01 — Live Appraisal

Your score. Every day. No surprises.

Pulse calculates every employee's appraisal score in real-time — not just at the end of a cycle. Employees always know where they stand, what's driving their score, and exactly what they need to do to improve.

  • Weighted scoring across goals, KPIs, reports, peer feedback, and manager assessment
  • Every component is visible and explainable to the employee
  • Performance-linked compensation displayed in plain numbers
  • Configurable weights per department and cadre level

02 — AI Report Intelligence

AI reads every report so managers don't have to miss anything.

Employees write. AI extracts. Managers act. Pulse's AI processes every qualitative report and surfaces accomplishments, blockers, goal references, and sentiment — mapped to the employee's live appraisal score.

  • Extracts key accomplishments and maps them to company OKRs
  • Flags repeated blockers before they become performance issues
  • Detects early sentiment signals — engagement, stress, disengagement
  • Manager sees a structured digest — raw report always one tap away

03 — One Portal, Every Role

One login. The right view for every person.

Pulse adapts to every employee automatically. Entry-level staff, team leads, senior managers, HR admins, and executives — all get exactly the information and tools relevant to their role. No separate portals. No switching between systems.

✦ How it works

Up and running in a week

We onboard your organisation, set up your goals and appraisal framework, and invite your team. You're live in days, not months.

1
We onboard you
Your HR admin configures the org structure, appraisal weights, leave types, and goal framework. We help you get it right from day one.
2
Staff get invited
Every employee receives a personalised invite. First login takes 3 minutes — they confirm their details and they're in their dashboard.
3
Goals go live
Managers set goals from the top down. Org → Department → Team → Individual. Every employee sees exactly what they're working toward and why it matters.
4
Reports flow in
Employees submit weekly reports. AI processes them instantly. Managers get digests, not novels. Scores update automatically.
